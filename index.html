CUBETERRA

A voxel survival sandbox. Original art, built from scratch.

  • Touch: left thumb moves, drag the right side to look. Tap to place, hold to mine. Buttons on the right do the same.
  • Keyboard: WASD move, Space jump, Shift sprint, click mine, right-click place, E inventory, 1-9 hotbar.
  • Start: punch a tree for logs, craft planks, a crafting table, then a pickaxe. Dig for coal and iron, smelt in a furnace.
  • Build: torches light caves, doors toggle, beds set spawn and skip night, sand and gravel fall, dug-out water flows. Axes and shovels dig wood and soil faster.
  • Explore: villages have houses, wells, roads and loot chests. Right-click chests, furnaces and crafting tables.
  • Night: zombies come out in the dark and in caves. Hold a torch to light your way.
